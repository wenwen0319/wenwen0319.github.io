Work in Progress

A Unified Framework for Multi-Turn Vegetation Authoring

Work in progress

An interactive framework for editing vegetation layouts over multiple turns. It combines reference-distribution controls with direct controls over effects such as visibility and shading, while retaining earlier goals and placement constraints as new edits are introduced. Users can review results and explicitly revise preferences to guide subsequent edits.
